Push / Pull Logbook

What LP, RP and DS mean
LP Lengthened partials
When you can’t do another full rep, keep going with 3–5 half reps in the stretched part of the movement, like the bottom half of a curl or press.
RP Rest-pause
After failure, rest 15 seconds, then do as many reps as you can with the same weight. Do this once more.
DS Drop set
After failure, lower the weight by about 25–30% and go to failure again straight away.
